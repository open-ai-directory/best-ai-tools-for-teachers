## How tools get listed

- **Live product only.** No waitlists, no "coming soon."
- **Public pricing or a real free tier.** If a vendor is enterprise-sales-only, we still list it, tagged `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`, so you know what you're getting into before you book a demo.
- **Actually used by teachers in the classroom**, not a general-purpose tool with one relevant use case bolted on.
- **Reviewed quarterly.** Dead links and abandoned products are removed — see [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- **No pay-to-list.** Sponsored placements are labeled `Sponsored` and never displace a better-fit free entry — see [Sponsors](#sponsors).

*Last full review: August 2026.*

## AI Tools for Lesson Planning

*AI lesson-planning tools turn a topic or standard into a full lesson, unit, or set of teaching materials in minutes.*

- **[Eduaide.ai](https://www.eduaide.ai/)** — Generates lesson plans, activities, and assessments from 100+ templates built on frameworks like UbD and 5E. `Freemium`
- **[Flint](https://flintk12.com/teachers)** — K-12 platform where teachers build lesson plans and monitored AI practice spaces from uploaded materials. `Freemium`
- **[Khanmigo for Teachers](https://www.khanmigo.ai/teachers)** — Khan Academy's teacher assistant drafts lesson plans, rubrics, exit tickets, and student-work summaries. `Free`
- **[MagicSchool AI](https://www.magicschool.ai/)** — Packages 80+ generators for lesson plans, quizzes, IEPs, and parent letters into one teacher dashboard. `Freemium`
- **[TeacherMatic](https://teachermatic.com/)** — UK-based platform with 150+ AI generators for lesson plans, schemes of work, and feedback. `Freemium`
- **[Twee](https://twee.com/)** — Generates dialogues, reading questions, and worksheets for English-language teachers from any topic. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Grading & Feedback

*AI grading tools score student writing and assignments against a rubric and draft feedback for a teacher to review before it goes out.*

- **[Class Companion](https://classcompanion.com/)** — Scores short-answer and essay responses against a rubric and gives students instant, coached feedback. `Freemium`
- **[CoGrader](https://cograder.com/)** — Imports Google Classroom essays, grades them against a custom rubric, and pushes grades back automatically. `Freemium`
- **[EssayGrader](https://www.essaygrader.ai/)** — Grades uploaded essays against a rubric in about 30 seconds and flags likely AI-written submissions. `Freemium`
- **[Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/)** — Groups similar exam and problem-set answers so instructors grade one example and apply it to matches. `Paid`
- **[Grammarly for Education](https://www.grammarly.com/edu)** — Institutional writing assistant that checks grammar, clarity, and tone for student and staff writing. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Writable](https://www.writable.com/)** — K-12 writing platform that drafts rubric-aligned comments and AI scores on student essays for teacher review. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Quiz & Assessment Generation

*AI assessment tools turn a topic, PDF, or slide deck into a ready-to-assign quiz or interactive check for understanding.*

- **[Conker](https://www.conker.ai/)** — Generates multiple-choice, true/false, and short-answer quizzes from a topic, PDF, article, or YouTube video. `Freemium`
- **[Formative](https://www.formative.com/)** — Live-response assessment platform whose Luna AI assistant drafts standards-aligned activities from a prompt or document. `Freemium`
- **[Kahoot!](https://kahoot.com/schools/)** — Turns a topic, PDF, or scanned notes into a game-show-style quiz for live or self-paced play. `Freemium`
- **[Wayground (formerly Quizizz)](https://wayground.com/)** — AI-generated quizzes, interactive video lessons, and analytics for teachers; rebranded from Quizizz in 2025. `Freemium`
- **[Wooclap](https://www.wooclap.com/en/)** — Quiz Wizard generates a short quiz from a topic or uploaded document for live classroom polling. `Freemium`
- **[Yippity](https://yippity.io/)** — Turns pasted notes, a PDF, or a link into editable practice questions teachers can share or export. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Differentiated Instruction & Reading Levels

*AI differentiation tools rewrite the same text at multiple reading levels or generate individualized goals for diverse learners.*

- **[Brisk Teaching](https://www.briskteaching.com/)** — Chrome extension that levels, translates, and quizzes any Google Doc, Slide, or web article for a class. `Freemium`
- **[Diffit](https://web.diffit.me/)** — Turns any text, topic, or video into leveled reading passages with vocabulary and comprehension questions. `Freemium`
- **[Goalbook Toolkit](https://goalbook.com/toolkit)** — Goal bank and UDL strategy library that helps special education teachers write IEP goals. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Newsela](https://newsela.com/)** — Adapts current news and nonfiction articles to multiple reading levels with built-in quizzes and translations. `Freemium`
- **[Quill.org](https://www.quill.org/)** — Nonprofit platform giving students AI-diagnosed grammar and writing practice with teacher progress reports. `Freemium`
- **[Read&Write](https://www.everway.com/products/read-and-write-education/)** — Literacy toolbar with text-to-speech, word prediction, and text simplification for struggling readers. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Parent Communication

*AI communication tools translate, draft, and route messages between teachers and families across languages and channels.*

- **[Bloomz](https://www.bloomz.com/)** — Messaging and classroom-activity app for teachers and families with two-way translation in 250+ languages. `Freemium`
- **[ClassDojo](https://www.classdojo.com/)** — Classroom messaging and photo-sharing app with AI-assisted translated SMS to families in 30+ languages. `Freemium`
- **[Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/)** — Meeting transcription tool teachers use to record and summarize parent-teacher conferences; offers a .edu discount. `Freemium`
- **[ParentSquare](https://www.parentsquare.com/parentsquare-ai/)** — District communication platform whose AI rewrite tool simplifies message readability and auto-translates 190+ languages. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[SchoolStatus Connect](https://www.schoolstatus.com/products/connect)** — District family-engagement platform (formerly ClassTag) with automatic message translation and data-driven outreach. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Smore](https://www.smore.com/)** — Newsletter builder with AI drafting for classroom updates, event announcements, and translated family messages. `Freemium`
- **[TalkingPoints](https://talkingpts.org/)** — Two-way texting app that auto-translates teacher-family messages across roughly 150 languages. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Instructional Coaching & Professional Development

*AI coaching tools analyze classroom recordings or observation notes to give teachers and coaches targeted, framework-aligned feedback.*

- **[Edthena (AI Coach)](https://www.edthena.com/ai-coach-for-teachers/)** — Browser-based virtual coach that analyzes uploaded classroom video and prompts self-paced teacher reflection. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Observation Copilot](https://observationcopilot.com/)** — Turns a coach's pasted classroom-visit notes into structured, framework-aligned observation feedback. `Free`
- **[Observation Partner](https://observationpartner.com/)** — AI-assisted classroom observation feedback and observation tracking for K-12 school leaders. `Freemium`
- **[Sibme](https://www.sibme.com/)** — K-12 coaching platform for recording, sharing, and annotating classroom video with AI-assisted talk analysis. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Steplab](https://steplab.co/)** — Professional-development platform with an AI-driven coaching engine that scaffolds feedback sessions between coaches and teachers. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[TeachFX](https://teachfx.com/)** — Records classroom audio and reports teacher-vs-student talk time, question types, and academic vocabulary use. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Classroom Engagement & Interactive Lessons

*AI engagement tools turn static lesson content into live polls, quizzes, and interactive slides students respond to in real time.*

- **[ClassPoint AI](https://www.classpoint.io/)** — PowerPoint add-in that generates quiz questions from slide content and adds polls and annotations. `Freemium`
- **[Curipod](https://curipod.com/)** — Turns a topic into an interactive lesson with live polls, drawings, word clouds, and AI feedback on responses. `Freemium`
- **[Nearpod](https://nearpod.com/)** — Interactive slide platform with an AI question generator for live or self-paced formative checks. `Freemium`
- **[Pear Deck](https://www.peardeck.com/)** — Turns a topic or existing materials into interactive slide decks and gamified practice sets using generative AI. `Freemium`
- **[SchoolAI](https://schoolai.com/)** — Lets teachers build monitored AI chat "Spaces" for students with content boundaries and a live oversight dashboard. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Content Creation (Slides, Worksheets & Video)

*AI content tools generate classroom-ready slides, worksheets, digital books, and interactive video from a teacher's prompt or materials.*

- **[Adobe Express for Education](https://www.adobe.com/education/k12.html)** — Free K-12 design app with generative-AI image tools for making worksheets, posters, and slides. `Free`
- **[Book Creator](https://bookcreator.com/)** — Digital book-making app for classrooms with an integrated Adobe AI image generator for illustrations. `Freemium`
- **[Canva for Education](https://www.canva.com/education/)** — Free-for-K-12 design suite whose Magic Studio AI tools generate worksheets, slides, and images. `Free`
- **[Edpuzzle](https://edpuzzle.com/)** — Turns instructional video into an interactive lesson using an AI question generator and autograding. `Freemium`
- **[Gamma](https://gamma.app/)** — Generates presentations, documents, and simple web pages from a text prompt or outline. `Freemium`
- **[Nolej](https://nolej.io/)** — Converts uploaded video, text, or a URL into an interactive micro-lesson with quizzes, flashcards, and a glossary. `Paid`
- **[Slidesgo](https://slidesgo.com/ai)** — Template library with an AI presentation maker plus lesson-plan and quiz generators for educators. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Plagiarism & AI-Writing Detection

*AI detection tools scan student submissions for plagiarism and text likely generated by chatbots like ChatGPT.*

- **[Copyleaks](https://copyleaks.com/)** — LMS-integrated scanner that returns combined plagiarism and AI-detection results for student submissions. `Paid`
- **[GPTZero](https://gptzero.me/educators)** — Scans text for likely AI-generated content from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and other models. `Freemium`
- **[Originality.ai](https://originality.ai/education)** — Credit-based scanner with an Academic Model tuned for lower false-positive AI detection on student writing. `Paid`
- **[Turnitin](https://www.turnitin.com/)** — Institutional plagiarism and AI-writing detector integrated into LMS platforms like Canvas and Moodle. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Winston AI](https://gowinston.ai/ai-detector-for-education/)** — AI-writing detector with PDF reporting and batch upload built for teachers grading student essays. `Paid`
